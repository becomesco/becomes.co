About Becomes

Becomes is an independent design and technology studio. We make brands, products and experiences that get people involved, make them feel something and leave room for a few surprises.

We like giving people something to play with and a say in how it turns out. We want the result to feel a little more theirs because they helped shape it, whether it lives on a screen or ends up in their hands.

Alongside client work, we develop our own products and projects across community services, personal keepsakes and digital publishing. They’re a way to give something back to the communities we’re part of, using what we know about design, development, marketing and building businesses.
